# Cart Inventory Guardrails

## User Story

As a shopper, I want the cart to stop me from adding more units than are available in stock so that I do not place an order the store cannot fulfil.

## Summary

- Shows available stock on product detail pages.
- Adds out-of-stock messaging before adding to cart.
- Disables the plus control when displayed quantity reaches available stock.
- Adds backend validation for cart updates when the requested quantity exceeds product stock.
